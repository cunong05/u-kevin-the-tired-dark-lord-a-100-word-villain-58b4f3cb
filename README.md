# The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue

Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/c/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb) — a 100-word monologue spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. Prize pool: $2.

## The monologue

> Come in. Shut the door. Sit — no, not there. That chair holds my maps, my lists, the names of lords who owe me coin since the marsh war, thirty years gone. The bells ring at noon since I said so. If they stop, the mills stop. If the mills stop, the bread stops. Then they all look at me. The plan is plain. At the feast, the salt. In the salt, the draught. One cup for my brother the king, and the crown comes home. To me. At last. ...Where was I? Ah. The salt. Yes. Mind the step.

Exactly 100 words. The tiredness is never announced — it lives in the pauses, the trailing off ("...Where was I?"), and the small deflections — while the speaker stays a villain with a history (the marsh war, thirty years of debts) and a scheme (the draught in the salt at the feast, one cup for his brother the king).

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 481 characters — inside the gate's 1–600 character limit) and prints it to stdout.
